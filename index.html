🌈 Hi, I'm Qixin Wang（王琦鑫）

Ph.D. Student · National University of Singapore

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore, advised by Professor Khoo Eng Tat. I am exploring affective avatars, human-computer interaction, and interaction in virtual reality. I am interested in how embodied, emotionally expressive agents and immersive environments can make human-computer interaction more natural, expressive, and engaging.

I received my master’s degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, advised by Professor Jia Jia. My research there focused on human-computer interaction, multimodal speech synthesis, and affective computing. In my master’s thesis, I worked on a video accessibility system for blind and low-vision (BLV) users based on multimodal agent collaboration.

My path into interaction research began at Beihang University, where I studied Digital Media Art, advised by Professor Chong Cao, and built VR experiences and interactive digital art. That hands-on work with immersive media still shapes my interest in how people feel, express, and connect in virtual spaces.
